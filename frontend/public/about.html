About this portal

The NTC Guest Satisfaction Portal collects feedback from members of the public who visit NTC offices or use NTC services. Responses are reviewed by the Guest Satisfaction team and summarized for management reporting.

The survey takes about 2–4 minutes and covers service quality, staff courtesy, facility accessibility, and your overall experience. Your responses help identify where service can be improved.
